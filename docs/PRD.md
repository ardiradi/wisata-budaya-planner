## 1. Outcome, users and scope

The product helps travelers produce editable cultural and culinary itineraries with traceable facts and computable feasibility. The [Product Brief](PRODUCT_BRIEF.md) records persona hypotheses, product boundaries and success measures. The pilot must support a visitor, a cultural explorer, a small-group organizer, a traveler with explicit food or access needs, and a trusted content steward.

The primary outcome is a plan the traveler can inspect and adapt while understanding uncertainty. Generation volume alone is not a success metric. Report validated, draft, blocked, failed and abandoned outcomes separately, including the reason for every incomplete result.

Booking, payment, public sharing, collaborative editing, public content submission, live tracking, unsupported transport modes, native apps and unrestricted AI destination discovery are excluded. No document in this package establishes specific Palembang venue facts.

### Fact and result semantics

- `verified`: a field is supported by approved evidence, within its freshness policy and without an unresolved conflict. Verification describes editorial evidence review, not perpetual truth.
- `estimated`: a permitted numerical estimate derived by a deterministic, reviewed method with visible assumptions and version. Never apply this to identity, history or opening hours.
- `unknown`: supporting information is missing or insufficient. Absence of a price is not a free price; absence of hours is not an all-day opening window.
- `stale`: evidence exceeded its freshness window or a known update requires review. Historical claims can have a different review policy from operational hours and prices.
- `conflicted`: credible records disagree on a field and the discrepancy has not been resolved. Do not silently choose the more convenient value.

Itinerary `validated` means every hard check passes under permitted disclosed estimates and current evidence snapshots. `draft` means incomplete evidence or scheduling prevents that conclusion; uncertain-hour venues remain unscheduled suggestions. `blocked` means a contradiction or unsatisfied hard constraint prevents a complete itinerary. A result may contain useful partial material, but its label and explanation must remain accurate. Validation status is separate from generation lifecycle.

## 4. Constraints validation specification

Run validation after intake, after candidate selection, before any result is labeled validated, after each edit, before saving, and when underlying evidence is invalidated. Validation reports contain a stable rule ID, severity, affected item/field, human-readable reason, relevant source/policy version and possible user-controlled resolution.

| Rule family | Hard rule and required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and geography | Every scheduled place has an active approved identity, matching city and allowed coordinates; no free-text or unknown IDs. Retired or revoked records cannot be newly scheduled. |
| Dates and time | Dates are in the configured planning horizon; all stops and travel legs fit daily availability and supported same-day windows. Use city-local time for rules and retain timezone identity with timestamps. |
| Opening and exceptions | Every scheduled activity's entire required visit interval fits a reviewed, date-applicable opening interval, including exceptions, closures, last-admission rules and booking requirements where applicable. Evidence freshness is distinct from validity for the travel date. If the evidence does not cover the future date or relevant exceptions, the stop cannot be validated. Unknown, stale or conflicted opening evidence prevents validated scheduling. Do not fabricate holiday behavior. |
| Travel feasibility | A supported route exists for every scheduled adjacent leg and both day anchors on a scheduled day; an explicit free day without modeled travel has no required route legs. Each result/cache key matches exact directed origin/destination access-point identities and coordinates, mode, departure assumption and time bucket; the reverse direction or another entrance is not interchangeable. Reordering, access-point changes, mode changes and shifted departure buckets invalidate affected legs. Travel durations and buffers use permitted, freshness-checked route data or an explicitly approved fallback; missing routing cannot silently become a zero-minute leg. An unapproved fallback yields draft or blocked. |
| Visit duration and pace | Use reviewed minimum and target durations by place/category. Do not compress below a minimum to make a plan fit. Estimated durations and rest/meal policy must be visible. At most 5 scheduled venue stops per day, excluding breaks. |
| Food and access needs | Every mandatory requirement has affirmative compatible evidence at the required specificity. Ambiguity, unknown cross-venue applicability or a contradictory source excludes the option; explain that a plan cannot establish suitability when evidence is missing. |
| Mandatory interests | Each explicitly required category has at least one eligible scheduled matching visit across the trip, based on a reviewed category assignment. A visit covers more than one category only through reviewed assignments for each; real taxonomy, overlap and individual classification approval remain pending independent cultural review. Unscheduled suggestions and broad daily culture/food coverage do not satisfy it. Known missing coverage is blocked; missing classification evidence yields draft. Only an explicit request revision can remove a requirement. |
| Walking | Sum conservative upper meters per traveler/day for all nonoverlapping walking components: walking route segments, venue interiors, access/transfer/pickup connectors and both anchors. Every occurrence counts; the same covered span is not counted twice within an occurrence. Include walking in car/taxi trips. Compare against the selected pace/user limit, without party-size multiplication. Unknown required components prevent validation. |
| Authoritative evidence | Resolve each field/scope through its explicit publication head and current ordered review event, then evaluate freshness, rights, revocation and travel-date applicability. Never search backward for an older verified record when the head is unknown, stale, conflicted or revoked. Unpublished drafts do not replace a head. Pin head/review revisions in candidates and recheck atomically before committing. See Data Model and M1 contract decisions. |
| Budget | Sum all included cost components using declared quantity bases, party size, rounding and estimate-policy version. Include confirmed necessary meals even without a scheduled food venue; use reviewed deterministic allowances or explicit traveler-provided/prepaid meal declarations with visible incremental-cost scope. Do not omit a meal to fit the budget. For a range, use the upper bound; add a disclosed 10% contingency, rounded upward to integer IDR, before the hard budget check. Exceeding the user budget is blocked. Missing any required component or necessary allowance evidence yields draft because affordability cannot be established. Unknown required cost cannot be treated as zero. A zero destination price requires explicit evidence or a permitted reviewed estimate policy; a traveler-provided meal declaration is a separate input assumption. |
| Edit and lock consistency | Preserve locked stops unless the traveler unlocks them. Locks do not override evidence revocation, impossible dates or hard constraints; report the conflict instead. Reordering recalculates routes, costs and every downstream time. |
| Completeness | A validated result must satisfy the requested trip and declared mandatory requirements without omitted days, required anchor legs or necessary cost categories. Each day includes at least one cultural and one culinary experience unless the traveler explicitly opts out of a category for that day or marks a free day. Required meal coverage still applies after such an opt-out. Insufficient availability yields draft/block, never a vacuous empty validated day. Free time is allowed and explicit; incomplete modeled days/plans use draft or blocked with precise gaps. A partial arrival/departure day can validate when all applicable requirements and its confirmed necessary-meal inventory pass. |

The application can present suggested input changes, such as a different day, a higher budget, fewer stops or removing a hard requirement. Apply a change only after the traveler explicitly selects it. Do not present a partial plan as validated by changing the meaning of the request. Sparse routing/lower density is an approved search preference, not permission to remove a required or locked stop or change a requested trip shape. Route attempts, including access work, retries and recalculation, share the unchanged 42-attempt ceiling.

## 7. AI, source and editorial policies

The AI boundary, provider tradeoffs and orchestration are specified in [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md). The [Data Model](DATA_MODEL.md) defines canonical records and evidence relationships. The model may rank/select known candidates and return permitted structured references; it does not supply the facts rendered to travelers. Deterministic services own scheduling, price arithmetic and status assignment. Reject malformed or out-of-allowlist output rather than repairing it with invented fields.

An editorial source register must document authority, scope, usage rights, attribution, review responsibility and caching/retention restrictions. Source hierarchy is field-specific: an official venue schedule may support hours while a heritage reference may support a historical claim. A source is not automatically authoritative for every field. Conflicts require explicit review; a source fetch time alone is not verification. Exact freshness windows remain a launch-blocking policy decision.

Every published claim needs approved display text, its evidence links and review status. Paraphrases/translations require review and traceability. Numerical estimates need deterministic methods, coverage and limitations; AI guesses are prohibited. Do not scrape or retain provider content beyond approved rights. Operational changes flag affected plans and suppress withdrawn claims/unusable directions in their current presentation, including historical version views. Purge affected traveler-facing projections/caches; retain only lawful restricted internal snapshots with an explicit withdrawal marker. ## 8. Privacy, security and consent behavior

Collect only what the plan needs. Food/access inputs are optional and may be sensitive in context; explain why they are used, avoid inference and exclude them from marketing or profiling. Free text is bounded, untrusted and minimized; use controlled fields where possible. Do not send traveler names, email addresses or unnecessary precise anchors to the model. Provider processors, data region, lawful basis, consent requirements and cross-border terms need review before live data use; this plan does not assert legal compliance.

The UI exposes retention, deletion, provider use, estimate meaning and location handling in plain Indonesian. Optional analytics/marketing must not be necessary for generation. A guest cannot recover expired content through a guessable identifier. Editorial tooling requires stronger authentication and least privilege. Store secrets outside client bundles and redact them in failures; citations and fetched source text are untrusted input.

## 9. Testing and rollout

Use unit and property-based tests for deterministic rules, schema/contract tests for provider adapters, integration tests for storage and authorization, and end-to-end tests for the critical journeys in [User Flows](USER_FLOWS.md). Keep curated golden fixtures separate from live facts and clearly label them synthetic. Test source injection as data, ID substitutions, date/time boundary arithmetic, pricing quantity bases, concurrent edits and restore/deletion behavior. Vitest domain/local-adapter and revision integration plus Playwright synthetic generation/edit/preview/commit/revert tests are authorized for B2. Cover locked/stale/concurrent edits, mandatory coverage, necessary meals, budget/time changes, missing/revoked evidence, directed-route recomputation and retained history. Real-provider, database authorization and durable restore/deletion suites remain obligations for separately reviewed phases, not current claims.

Release through internal synthetic-data review, editorial staging with approved source rights, separately authorized capped provider integration tests, moderated user testing, and an invite-only pilot before wider availability. The retained M5 catalogue target is 30 distinct approved places, including at least 10 food venues and at least 3 cultural-interest categories; smaller M1 samples are not a launch-scope change. Feature flags can disable generation or a provider without taking away private saved-plan access. Monitor quality before expanding catalog breadth or city count. Milestone gates and ownership are defined in [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md).

## 10. Risks and unresolved product decisions

| Risk or decision | Required resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Insufficient reliable opening, price, access or dietary evidence | Audit coverage against golden scenarios and complete curation. A narrower supported experience requires a separate explicit product decision; never lower evidence standards to hit an inventory count. |
| Historical/cultural misrepresentation | Name content reviewers; approve claim scope, respectful terminology, translation and correction procedures. |
| Travelers mistake estimates for guarantees | Validate wording and comprehension; show scope and assumptions beside the relevant value. |
| Provider cost or restrictions prevent proposed routing | Approve service choice, allowable storage, monetary caps, cache policy and failover behavior before implementation. |
| Budget uncertainty makes a whole trip impossible to validate | Approve deterministic cost policies and upper-bound treatment; block/draft when a necessary cost remains unknown. |
| Limits exclude valuable users | Validate usability and feasibility of preserved 1–7-day support and the remaining proposed intake defaults. Research does not change approved scope or ceilings without a new decision. |
| Saved plans outlive source validity | Approve revalidation rules and user notice; distinguish historical validation from current status. |
| Privacy, region, retention and backup capabilities | Verify hosting/provider options and legal requirements; adjust approved service promises before launch. |
| Operating load outgrows content capacity | Assign review owners, refresh queues and incident coverage; expand only when each city's maintenance is funded. |
