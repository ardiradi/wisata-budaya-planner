## Method, status and measurable gate

Each scenario below is an invented **audit input**, not a real traveler request, recommendation, booking, availability claim or production fixture of verified facts. Only input dates, windows, party counts, chosen budget limits, priorities and declared meal assumptions are synthetic. Real venue names are research leads with source links, never synthetic evidence for their operational facts. No coordinates, route geometry, journey times, menu prices, tariffs, opening exceptions, historical chronology or reviewer approvals have been invented.

The three scenarios collectively exercise the three proposed modes: `walking`, `private_car`, `taxi_ride_hail`. One deliberately requires Ilir–Ulu movement across the Musi barrier. Coverage of a mode as an audit input is not proof that the provider or local network supports it. No ferry, motorcycle, transit or other unapproved mode is substituted.

The measurable **audit-completeness criterion** is 3/3 one-day records, collectively covering 3/3 proposed modes and at least one geographic barrier, with an explicit evidence/status entry for identity, exact location/access, weekly hours/date exceptions/last entry, group costs, all meals, both anchors/return, route and internal walking, mandatory interests and promised suitability. An unavailable source must be labeled rather than accepted from a snippet. This document meets that desk-audit criterion.

That is distinct from the **M1 exit gate**: named operating owners, approved source/rights/current-assertion policies, actual independently approved sample records, and measured route tests covering every supported mode and the barrier, alongside the engineering/retention gates in [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md). Those mandatory evidence items remain pending. The baseline's **three fully feasible, locally reviewed one-day scenarios** remains an M5 launch gate: all applicable fields, meal/group budgets, walking, interests, hard needs and route legs must pass then, with recorded human approval. Advancing that entire M5 gate into M1 would be a new product decision; this audit does not silently do so.

Research states: `desk_found` = source lead, `missing` = no adequate inspected support, `conflicted` = incompatible inspected source statements, `review_pending` = source exists but required authority/rights/human review is absent. These states do not replace the product's `verified | estimated | unknown | stale | conflicted` field states. In particular, **zero fields are promoted to verified by this audit**. Undated evidence has unknown freshness; it is not assigned a fabricated review date or asserted stale merely because it is undated.
