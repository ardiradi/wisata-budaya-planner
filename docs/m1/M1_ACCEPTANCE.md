## Evidence classes and status

- **DOC:** a primary public page was actually opened, with exact URL, retrieval date, scope and limitations. It does not prove account configuration, legal permission or real-world conditions.
- **LOCAL:** reproducible synthetic experiment, exact command/runtime/input and machine-readable output. Simulated provider time/rates are labeled separately from wall-clock measurements.
- **HUMAN:** identified real reviewer, relevant competence, distinct authorship, specific signed/recorded scope/date and source review. AI output never supplies this evidence.
- **LIVE:** separately approved real provider/hosting/database test, with request settings, usage/charges, timing and sanitized results. No such tests are authorized by current M1 approval.
- **PASS:** the specified evidence exists and meets the criterion. **FAIL:** tested or documented evidence contradicts a required capability. **PENDING:** required proof/decision is absent. Not attempted is pending, not pass. Expected safe rejection in a negative local test can pass that test while the proposed recovery capability fails.
