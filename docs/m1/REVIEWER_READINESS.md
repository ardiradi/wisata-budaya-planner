## 2. Recruitment brief — draft text for later authorized use

**Role:** Independent evidence reviewer, Palembang pilot, Wisata Budaya Planner.

**Purpose:** Check whether each proposed place fact, estimate and itinerary claim is supported by current, appropriately authoritative evidence for its exact scope. Identify what cannot be approved. Review cultural context respectfully, keep verified facts separate from estimates and preserve uncertainty. Fluent prose, an official-looking listing or an AI answer does not establish truth.

**Proposed work:** Review contributor-prepared evidence bundles; check exact entity/branch and entrance identity; assess source authority and conflicts; check weekly hours and date exceptions; review group tariffs and necessary meal costing; verify taxonomy assignments; examine directed route/walking evidence within documented competence; record decisions, expiry and corrections. Escalate dietary, accessibility and rights questions outside the agreed scope. Review is separate from publication, product decisions and engineering implementation.

**Initial engagement:** A bounded, owner-authorized sample exercise followed by a capacity review. The present 2–3 hours/week target is a recruitment hypothesis. The model below indicates it cannot cover an ordinary 30-place review queue. No rate, payment, contract, start date, applicant or available person is asserted.

**Requested professional evidence:** Examples of relevant Palembang cultural/local research or editorial work; an explanation of source verification methods; a practical local-access/field-evidence plan; a limited conflict-of-interest declaration; and actual weekly/holiday availability. Examples may be redacted or use public professional references. Do not request identity documents, home address, birth date, religion, medical information or a personal travel history.

**Authority boundary:** Appointment authorizes only its recorded field/city/scope. It never grants permission to approve one's own work, invent missing facts, waive hard constraints, pronounce blanket halal/allergy/accessibility suitability, interpret all licences conclusively, provision services or publish a live catalogue.

This text is prepared for the user to review. **Do not send it, search for/contact candidates, contact references or make a paid engagement under this document.** External messages, visits, specialist work and payments need a separate scoped instruction and, where applicable, an agreed ceiling.
